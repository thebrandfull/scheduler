# Feb 1 Game Plan Tracker

A comprehensive tracking and reminder web application for your personal transformation journey.

## Features

- **Daily Schedule Tracking**: Track your daily tasks across fitness, cardio, Instagram, career, and mindset
- **Weekly Planning**: View and manage your week-by-week goals across Body, Mind, Instagram, and Career
- **Gym Program**: Complete workout program with exercises, sets, reps, and notes
- **Instagram Calendar**: Plan and track your Instagram content strategy
- **Habit Tracker**: Monitor your daily habits and build streaks
- **Nutrition Guide**: Access your nutrition plan
- **Content Hooks**: Store and organize your content ideas
- **Progress Dashboard**: Visual progress tracking with statistics and streaks
- **Persistent Storage**: All your progress is saved locally using localStorage
- **Simple Authentication**: PIN-based login to protect your data
- **Responsive Design**: Works seamlessly on both desktop and mobile devices

## Getting Started

### Default Login
- **Default PIN**: `1234`
- You can change your PIN in the Settings page after logging in

### Navigation
- Use the top navigation bar to switch between different sections
- Navigate through dates using the Previous/Next buttons in each view
- Check off tasks as you complete them to track your progress

## Usage

### Dashboard
- View your overall progress, current week info, and today's tasks
- See your habit streaks and quick statistics
- Visual progress ring shows your completion percentage

### Daily Schedule
- Navigate through each day of your plan
- Check off tasks in categories: Fitness, Cardio/Steps, Instagram, Career/Clarity, and Mindset
- Track completion for each day

### Weekly View
- See your weekly goals broken down by category
- Mark entire weeks as complete
- Review focus areas for each week

### Gym Program
- View all exercises with sets, reps, and rest periods
- Track completed workouts
- Access exercise notes and tips

### Instagram Calendar
- Plan your daily Instagram content
- Track posts, reels, carousels, and engagement activities
- Mark content as completed

### Habit Tracker
- Monitor daily habits and routines
- Build streaks by completing consecutive days
- View habit completion rates

### Settings
- Change your PIN for security
- Export your progress data as JSON
- Reset all progress if needed

## Data Persistence

All your progress is automatically saved to your browser's localStorage:
- Task completions
- Habit tracking
- Custom PIN
- No server or internet connection required after initial load

## Mobile Support

The app is fully responsive and optimized for:
- Smartphones (iOS and Android)
- Tablets
- Desktop browsers
- Portrait and landscape orientations

## Browser Compatibility

Works on all modern browsers:
- Chrome/Edge
- Firefox
- Safari

## Privacy

- All data is stored locally in your browser
- No data is sent to any server
- Your information stays on your device
- Clearing browser data will reset the app
